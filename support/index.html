Common questions

Which model should I use?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, it's ready with no download. For more capable answers, open Settings › Downloaded model and get Qwen3 4B, which is the best balance of speed and smarts on a phone. Bigger models need more memory and are slower.

The first answer is slow

A downloaded model takes a few seconds to load after Doko opens. Answers after that are faster.

Doko can't hear me on a call

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are on in the iPhone's Settings › Apps › Doko. If Doko hears its own voice, turn off Clearer voice in Doko's Settings.

Weather, places or reminders don't work

These need permission for Location, Calendar, Reminders or Contacts. Turn them on in Settings › Apps › Doko.

How do I free up space?

In Doko's Settings › Downloaded model, tap Delete on a model you don't use. The natural voices can be removed with Delete natural voices in Settings.

Your data

Doko keeps your chats on your iPhone and we don't collect any data, so there is no account to delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. More in the Privacy Policy.
