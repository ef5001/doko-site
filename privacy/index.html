Privacy Policy

Doko — a private assistant for iPhone. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

The short version: Doko has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your chats, documents and settings stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers. Some features need the internet, like weather, maps and web searches. For those, Doko sends only what that request needs, straight from your phone to the service that answers it.

Doko is made by Chaotic Neutral LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Doko does with your information. You can reach us about privacy at {{EMAIL}}.

1. What stays on your iPhone

  • Conversations. Your chats and call transcripts are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting a chat or the app removes them.
  • Thinking. Answers come from a model you choose. Apple Intelligence and Downloaded models run entirely on your iPhone. If you pick Your computer, your messages go to the computer address you entered (for example LM Studio or Ollama on your home network), and nowhere else.
  • Voice calls. What you say is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The natural voices also run on your iPhone. No audio is recorded or uploaded.
  • Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and Music. Doko reads or adds to these only when you ask, and only after you give permission. The details are used to answer you on your phone.
  • Documents. PDFs, Word files and spreadsheets Doko makes are created and saved on your iPhone.
  • Keys. Any optional keys you enter, like a Brave Search key, are kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

2. What leaves your iPhone, and why

These requests go directly from your iPhone to the service named. None of them includes your name, contacts, messages or an account. Like any internet request, each one reveals your device's IP address to that service.

3. Location

With your permission, Doko uses your location only while you're using it. It uses it for local weather, nearby places and your time zone. Your location is used on your iPhone. Only what a request needs is sent, as described above: rounded coordinates for weather, an approximate position for nearby places.

4. What we collect

Nothing. Chaotic Neutral LLC does not run servers for Doko and receives no data from the app. Doko has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash-reporting services or tracking, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics and crash reports, if you chose to share them with app developers in your iPhone's settings. These do not identify you.

5. Your choices

  • Turn any permission (location, microphone, speech recognition, calendar, reminders, contacts, music) on or off in the iPhone's Settings › Apps › Doko.
  • Delete chats in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored, including downloaded models.
  • Pick a model that runs on your iPhone if you don't want messages to leave it.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Your rights under laws like the GDPR and the CCPA still apply. Contact us with any question about them. We do not sell or share personal information.

6. Children

Doko is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. AI answers

Doko's answers are made by AI models and can be wrong. Don't rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. See our Terms of Use.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If Doko ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app first.
